- **A documented incident — correcting a day heading temporarily lost a task line:** a request to correct a day heading in a weekly tracker file. A first attempt with `patch_vault_file(heading, replace, expectedContent)` failed with `stale_precondition` although the content seemed to match (most likely a subtle whitespace difference). A second attempt without `expectedContent` succeeded technically but **lost the whole task line** and duplicated the heading (empty twice). A third attempt to repair the mess with another `patch_vault_file` also failed and doubled the duplication. **The successful fix:** a full `get_vault_file`, then `create_vault_file` with the full correct content + `expectedContent` to protect against overwriting any invisible change. **The decisive lesson:** to correct a simple section heading that carries content under it, `patch_vault_file` with `heading` is a risk out of proportion to the task — for precise text corrections (renaming a heading, a word, a date) use `search_and_replace` (with `dry_run: true` first) because it targets only the matching text with no section bounds, or `rename_heading` specifically to change a heading's text (it updates links too). `patch_vault_file(heading)` is kept for the cases where we really need to replace/add content **inside** the bounds of a whole section.

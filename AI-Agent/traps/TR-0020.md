Found during a vault-wide review after a file rename, in a weekly review note.

- A rename of a note rewrote links at wrong offsets inside two lines, producing text such as `word[[Renamed-Note]]eer BI]]` and `word[[Renamed-Note]]rest`.
- The corruption survived for three days: success messages and `find_broken_links` do not detect it.
- Fixed by restoring both lines verbatim from the earlier commit. A vault-wide scan for unbalanced brackets and brackets glued to letters found no other case.
- Balanced-bracket corruption is not detectable by pattern, so the git diff review of linking files is part of the rule.

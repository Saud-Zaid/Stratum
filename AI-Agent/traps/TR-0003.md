- `expectedContent` in `patch_vault_file` is effective only with `operation: replace` — it is ignored with `append`/`prepend` (no protection against overwriting a change I have not seen) → for any `append`/`prepend` edit on a file that may have changed, read it first with `get_vault_file` to be sure.

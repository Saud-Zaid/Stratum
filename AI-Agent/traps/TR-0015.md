Verified on `AI-Agent/fixtures/addressing.md` (T05 phase 1, 11 Sep 2026). Supersedes TR-0001, whose `prepend` claim ("inserted before the heading text") did not reproduce.

- `append` to `## Alpha` wrote the text after `### Alpha Child` and its body, i.e. at the end of the whole section including subsections.
- `prepend` to `Fixture Title::Alpha` wrote the text on the line directly under `## Alpha`, before the section's first body line.
- `replace` still covers the whole section up to the next heading of the same or higher level (unchanged from TR-0001).
- Targeting `Alpha` without the parent path works in files with and without an H1; `allowRootHeadings` was not needed.
- Before any heading write, read the exact target with `get_vault_file_partial` (same engine) to see the boundaries that will be affected.

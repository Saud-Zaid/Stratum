id: TR-0028
title: requireWritePreconditions makes expectedContent mandatory on replace and overwrite
kind: tool
tools:
- create_vault_file
- patch_vault_file
rule: 'With the connector setting requireWritePreconditions on, overwriting an existing file with create_vault_file, or patch_vault_file with operation replace, is refused unless expectedContent carries what you read. On stale_precondition: read the file again, then decide again; never resend the old expectedContent. Append, prepend and search_and_replace are not guarded (TR-0003): use append only for logs whose order does not matter.'

- **The reason:** the choice was delegated to the agent, and research showed that the setting turns a habit into a structure, and the connector's owner plans to make it the default in the next major release.
- **The cost:** rewriting a whole existing file (a script, for example) requires sending its current content in full as `expectedContent`, which doubles the size. Append and `search_and_replace` are not affected.
- **The comparison:** it ignores line endings, trailing spaces at the end of a line and blank lines at the start and the end, but blank lines inside the text and leading spaces count.
- **The recovery:** `stale_precondition` means someone wrote after your read (often the owner in the editor). Read again, see what changed, and decide again.

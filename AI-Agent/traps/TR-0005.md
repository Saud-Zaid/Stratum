- After any complex `patch_vault_file` or `create_vault_file` (a long heading, an edit in several places), verify at once with `search_vault_simple` for part of the added/changed text — checking is much cheaper than discovering a duplicate or an orphaned heading later. **Extra caution:** if more than one consecutive `patch_vault_file` attempt fails or leaves damage in the same file, stop patching and move to a full `get_vault_file` + a direct `create_vault_file` — do not repeat the same failing method.

Verified on `AI-Agent/fixtures/linker.md` (T05 phase 1, 11 Sep 2026).

- Renaming `### Alpha Child` returned `linkRewriteCount: 0` and left `[[addressing#Alpha#Alpha Child]]` pointing at a heading that no longer exists.
- Renaming `## Beta` rewrote `[[addressing#Beta]]` to `[[addressing#Beta Renamed]]` (`linkRewriteCount: 1`).
- The tool description claims subheading-path links are rewritten; in this vault they are not.

- `patch_vault_file` with `targetType: heading` and `operation: replace` or `prepend` works against the bounds of the **whole section up to the next heading of the same or a higher level**, not only the matching text; `prepend` in particular inserts the content **before the heading text itself**, not after it and before the first paragraph under it → to add content inside an existing section (not before its heading), target a finer sub-heading that sits inside the wanted section, or read the whole file and rewrite it with `create_vault_file`.

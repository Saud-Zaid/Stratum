- **Line numbers are 0-based in some tools and 1-based in others [11 Sep 2026, verified]:** `get_vault_file_partial` (`document-map`, `lines`) and `search_vault_simple` are 0-based; `get_note_outline` and vault-sandbox scripts (`section`, `outline`) are 1-based (same heading: 56 vs 57) → never pass a line number from one tool family to the other without converting; for a `lines` read based on a sandbox number, use start-1 and end-1.
